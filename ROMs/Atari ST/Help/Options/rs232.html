RS232

WinSTon allows communication via the RS-232 to other PCs and even a real ST. This can be used to connect multi-player games together such as 'Stunt Car Racer'.

NOTE - Some games/applications may not be able to communicate correctly with this feature. As newer versions of the emulator are released this compatibility will increase.



Enable RS-232

To connect two machine together enable this option. When disabled the emulator will assume no RS-232 lead is connected to the machine.

COM Port

You will need to select the name of the COM port which is connected to the other machine. Please ensure you enter the correct setting otherwise you may experience problems with your system.
