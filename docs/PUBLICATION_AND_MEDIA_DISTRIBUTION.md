## Comments and community interaction

The publication workflow does **not** automate bulk comments on unrelated YouTube videos. That would create repetitive unsolicited promotion and can be treated as spam. The safe workflow is to publish the user's own videos, add accurate descriptions/tags/links, and participate manually in genuinely relevant discussions. For relevant videos where a comment is appropriate, use a short non-repetitive disclosure such as:

> Chimera II OS is an open research OS/virtual-processor project exploring C8192/R8192 ISA, CPU4096 simulation, Aurora desktop and neural/128D research. Source: https://github.com/amerhwitat/ChimeraIIOS

## Publication record rule
